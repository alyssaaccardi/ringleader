## Hey team

This is the first issue of **Ringleader**, our internal newsletter for the Answering Legal crew. Anyone with an `@answeringlegal.com` Google account can sign in and read.

## How this works

- Every issue lives on this site under **Archive**.
- The **Current** tab always shows the latest issue.
- Sign-in is Google-only, gated to our domain — no passwords to manage.

## Publishing an issue

Issues are Markdown files committed to the repo. To publish a new one:

1. Drop a `.md` file into `content/issues/`
2. Push to `main`
3. `git pull && pm2 restart ringleader` on the VPS

That's it. See you in the next issue.
